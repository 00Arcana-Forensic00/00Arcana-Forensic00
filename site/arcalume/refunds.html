Arcalume refund policy

Effective date: [DATE OF FIRST RELEASE]

Every new install of Arcalume includes Pro free for 180 days, so you can try everything before you pay. If you buy Pro (a one-time US$5.99 purchase through Google Play) and it does not work out, here is how to get your money back.

Within 48 hours of buying

Request a refund directly from Google: open play.google.com, go to Payments & subscriptions > Budget & history, find the Arcalume purchase and choose Request a refund. Google decides these requests under the Google Play refund policy.

Within 30 days of buying

Email support@arcana-forensics.com with the order number from your Google Play receipt (it starts with "GPA."). We will refund the full price through Google Play, no questions asked, normally within 3 business days. The refund removes Pro from the Google account that bought it.

After 30 days

We look at each request individually, for example if Pro stopped working for you because of a problem we could not fix.

What a refund does not affect

Vaults you sealed and your custody log stay on your phone. They can always be opened and checked for free, with or without Pro.

Your legal rights

This policy adds to, and does not reduce, any rights you have under consumer law where you live, including any statutory right to withdraw from a digital purchase.
