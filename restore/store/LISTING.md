**Name:** Arcalume
**Subtitle (Mac App Store, 30 chars):** Recover glare-damaged pages
**Short description (Microsoft Store):** Bring back text lost to shadows and uneven light in photos of documents, see exactly what was filled in, and seal the original with the result. Everything stays on your device.

## Description

Took a photo of a receipt, contract or exhibit and the lamp washed half of it out? Arcalume
evens out shadows and uneven lighting across the page, restores faded text around glare
spots, and fills blown-out spots so they stop hiding the rest of the page.

It never pretends. Pixels that were pure white or pure black in your photo held no
information, so Arcalume cannot and does not "recover" text from them. It fills them
smoothly and marks every filled pixel, so you can see exactly what came from your photo
and what did not. Solid black areas, which are often redactions, are left alone unless
you ask.

- Compare before and after with a slider, and switch on "Show filled areas".
- Plain-language findings for every page: what was corrected, what was filled, what was left alone.
- Reading order for multi-column pages.
- Save a restored copy as PNG.
- **Pro:** seal the untouched original, the restored page and the fill map into one
  encrypted file (AES-256-GCM, Argon2id passphrase), and record it in a tamper-evident,
  hash-chained custody log you can check at any time.
- Opening sealed files and checking logs is always free.

**Private by design.** No account. No uploads. No analytics. The app makes no network
connections, and in the Mac App Store edition macOS enforces that.

**Accessible.** Full keyboard control, screen-reader labels, larger text up to 200%,
light and dark themes, high-contrast support, and filled areas shown with a pattern, not
only colour.
