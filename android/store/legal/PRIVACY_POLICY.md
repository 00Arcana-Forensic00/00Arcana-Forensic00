This policy covers the Arcalume app for Android, including test ("beta") builds. It is short
because the app does not collect data.

## The short version

Arcalume does not collect, receive, sell or share any personal data. The app has no internet
access, no account, no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising. Your photos, recovered
pages, vaults and custody log are processed and stored only on your phone.

## What stays on your phone

- **Photos you take or import.** Photos taken with the in-app camera, and images you choose with
  the system photo picker, file picker or the Android share menu, are copied into the app's
  private storage on your phone. They are processed there and never sent to us or anyone else.
- **Recovered pages, filled-pixel maps and reports** are created and kept in the same private
  storage.
- **Vaults and the custody log.** When you seal a page, the app encrypts it into a vault file and
  adds an entry to a custody log, both in the app's private storage. Vault file names and log
  entries do not contain the name of the original file.
- **Passphrases** you type to seal or open a vault are used on your phone to derive an encryption
  key. They are never stored and never sent anywhere. We cannot recover a forgotten passphrase.
- **Your plan.** The app stores whether Pro is unlocked, and the date of first launch so it can
  count the 180 days of free Pro. These stay on your phone.

The app's storage is excluded from Google cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer, so
none of this leaves your phone through a backup. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in
Android settings, deletes all of it. You can also delete individual vaults in the app.

## What leaves your phone, and only when you choose

- **Exports.** When you export a recovered page, a vault or the custody log, Android's "save as"
  dialog lets you choose where it goes (for example a folder on the phone or a cloud drive app
  you have installed). What happens to it there is governed by that place, not by us.
- **Copying the fingerprint** puts it on your phone's clipboard, where other apps you use may be
  able to read it.
- **Links in the app** (help, this policy, the terms) open in your web browser. Our website is
  hosted by Cloudflare, which processes standard request data such as IP addresses to serve and
  protect the site. The app itself sends nothing.

## Purchases

Pro is sold through Google Play. Google processes your payment and account details under the
Google Privacy Policy; we do not receive your card details, name or email address from a Play
purchase. The Play Store app tells Arcalume whether Pro was bought, together with a purchase token,
and the app stores only that Pro is unlocked. If you ask us for a refund or help with a purchase,
we will see the order details that Google Play Console shows us for that order, and use them only
to handle your request.

## Permissions

- **Camera**: only while you take a photo inside the app. You can refuse it and still import
  photos.
- **Billing** (added by Google Play Billing): to make the one-time Pro purchase.

Arcalume does not request access to your photo library, contacts, location, microphone or the
internet.

## Children

Arcalume is made for adults (professional and personal document work) and is not directed at
children under 13. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR (EU and UK) and the CCPA (California) give you rights to access,
correct, delete or move personal data an organisation holds about you, and to object to its use.
Because Arcalume does not collect personal data, we hold none from the app to act on. If you have
written to us, for example for support or a refund, you can ask us to access or delete that
correspondence at privacy@arcana-forensics.com. You can also complain to your local data
protection authority.

## Changes

If a future version of Arcalume changed what this policy says (for example by adding any feature
that sends data), we would update this policy and the Google Play Data safety section before
releasing that version, and change the effective date above.
