seal pages into encrypted vaults recorded in a hash-chained custody log. Please understand its

- It cannot recover text that a photo does not contain. Filled areas carry no original data, and
  the app marks them as such.
- The custody log shows whether records were changed after sealing. It cannot show what happened
  to a document or photo before it reached the app.
