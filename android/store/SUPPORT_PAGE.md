# Arcalume help and support

Email **support@arcana-forensics.com**. Please include your phone model and Android version, and
for purchases the order number from your Google Play receipt (it starts with "GPA.").

## Getting started

Open Arcalume and tap **Try a sample page** to see a recovery without using your own photos. Then
take a photo with the camera button, import one, or share an image to Arcalume from Photos, Files
or a mail app.

## Why are some areas hatched in magenta?

Those pixels were blown out by glare or shadow and held no data. Arcalume filled them smoothly so
the page is easier to read, and marks them so nobody mistakes them for recovered text. Arcalume
never invents characters. Retake the photo at a slight angle to capture text under a glare spot.

## I forgot my vault passphrase

A forgotten passphrase cannot be recovered. Arcalume never stores or sends passphrases, so
nobody, including us, can open that vault without it. If you still have the original photo, you
can recover and seal it again.

## Pro isn't unlocked after I bought it

Open the **Plan** tab and tap **Restore purchase** while the Play Store is signed in to the
Google account you bought with. If that does not work, email us your order number.

## How long is Pro free?

Every new install gets Pro free for 180 days from first launch. The Plan tab shows how many days
are left. After that you can keep Pro with one payment of US$5.99. Nothing is charged
automatically.

## Refunds

See the [refund policy](refunds.html). In short: within 48 hours, request it from Google Play;
within 30 days, email us and we refund in full.

## Does Arcalume upload my documents?

No. Arcalume does not request internet access, so it cannot upload anything. Read the
[privacy policy](privacy.html).
