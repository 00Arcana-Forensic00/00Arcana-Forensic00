# Messages for testers and customers

## Closed-test invitation (for the 12 testers / 14 days rule)

Personal developer accounts need at least 12 testers opted in to a closed test for 14 days in a
row before Play allows a production release. Send this to people with an Android phone and a
Google account, after adding their Gmail addresses to the closed-test track's email list.

```
Subject: Can you test my new Android app for two weeks?

Hi [name],

I'm launching Arcalume, an Android app that repairs photos of documents ruined by glare or
shadows and keeps a verifiable record of every change. Before Google lets me publish it, 12 people
need to test it for 14 days.

All you need to do:
1. Open this link on your Android phone and tap "Become a tester": [OPT-IN LINK]
2. Install Arcalume from the Play Store link on that page.
3. Keep it installed for 14 days, and open it a few times (try "Try a sample page").

It's free, there's no account, and the app has no internet access, so it can't see or send
anything from your phone. If anything looks wrong, just reply to this email.

Thank you!
[your name]
```

## Support replies

**Refund (within 30 days)**

```
Thanks for trying Arcalume, and sorry it wasn't right for you. I've refunded your Pro purchase
(order [GPA.xxxx]) through Google Play; you'll see it on your original payment method within a
few days. Your vaults stay on your phone and can still be opened and checked for free.
```

**Forgotten passphrase**

```
I'm sorry, a forgotten passphrase can't be recovered. Arcalume never stores or sends
passphrases, so nobody, including us, can open that vault without it. This is what keeps your
evidence private. If you still have the original photo, you can recover and seal it again with a
new passphrase.
```

**Pro not showing after buying**

```
Please open Arcalume, go to the Plan tab and tap "Restore purchase" while signed in to the Play
Store with the Google account you bought with. Arcalume itself has no internet access; the Play
Store app confirms the purchase. If it still doesn't unlock, send me your order number (it
starts with GPA.) and I'll check it.
```

**"Does Arcalume upload my documents?"**

```
No. Arcalume doesn't request internet access at all, so it can't upload anything. Photos,
vaults and the custody log stay in the app's private storage on your phone and are excluded
from cloud backups. They leave the phone only if you export them.
```

**"Will this hold up in court?"**

```
Arcalume marks every pixel it changed and seals the original next to the recovered copy, with a
custody log that shows whether anything was altered after sealing. Whether a court or insurer
accepts it is up to them; the app isn't a certification of admissibility, and I'd suggest asking
the lawyer or adjuster involved.
```
