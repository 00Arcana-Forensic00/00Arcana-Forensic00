### II. GLARING FACIAL DEFECTS & INTERNAL CONTRADICTIONS

#### 1. Incurable Contradiction Regarding Appearance and Due Process
* On **Page 1** of the Recommended Order, the Magistrate expressly finds:
  > *"The Petitioner and Respondent failed to appear."*
* Yet, on **Page 2, Paragraph 4**, the Magistrate makes an affirmative finding of fact based on purported live testimony:
  > *"The Respondent testified that he can pay $300.00 within thirty days."*
* **The Facial Contradiction:** A judicial officer cannot enter a binding finding that a litigant "failed to appear" and simultaneously adjudicate rights and enforce financial penalties based on purported statements that the party "testified." If Respondent was absent, the Court could not have taken sworn testimony regarding an ability to pay a $300.00 purge. If Respondent appeared remotely, the finding of failure to appear is fundamentally false and deprived Respondent of a recorded, meaningful hearing on his pending affirmative motions.

#### 2. False Finding That Respondent Did Not File a Response
* The Recommended Order states on Page 1: *"The Respondent did not file a response."*
* This statement is directly refuted by the official Marion County docket:
  * **Doc #113–115:** Motion to Terminate Child Support, Motion to Audit and Correct Arrears Ledger, and Emergency Motion for Temporary Stay of Enforcement.
  * **Doc #118:** Motion to Address Child Support Fraud.
  * **Doc #119 (Filed 09/05/2026):** Notice of Filing Combined Evidence Packet.
  * **Amended Notice of Filing (Exhibits D-1 & D-2):** Certified records from Brevard County establishing that maternal grandmother Sharon Overton has been the sole legal payee since 2021 and custody transferred in 2018.
* Entering an enforcement order while asserting on the face of the record that Respondent "did not file a response"—completely ignoring multiple pending, fully served substantive motions—constitutes a plain denial of procedural due process.

### III. SUBSTANTIVE & STATUTORY GROUNDS FOR VACATUR

#### 3. Unlawful Imposition of a Contempt "Purge" Without a Contempt Finding
* In **Paragraph 5**, the Magistrate commands:
  > *"The Respondent shall pay a purge of $300.00 within thirty days of the date of entry of this order."*
* Under black-letter Florida Supreme Court precedent (*Bowen v. Bowen*, 471 So. 2d 1274 (Fla. 1985)), a "purge" is exclusively a remedial sanction for **civil contempt**, requiring an express finding of willful refusal to pay and a present financial ability to pay the purge amount from non-exempt assets.
* The Court did **not** hold Respondent in willful civil contempt. In fact, Paragraph 5 concludes: *"Failure to comply with this order may result in further actions by the Court, including contempt proceedings."* Ordering a contempt "purge" before any contempt has ever been adjudicated is an error of law.

#### 4. Unlawful Compulsion Against Federally Protected VA Disability Compensation
* In **Paragraph 4**, the Court expressly finds:
  > *"The Respondent is unemployed and is receiving $1,748.00 per month in VA disability compensation."*
* Under **38 U.S.C. § 5301**, Veterans Affairs disability compensation is federally exempt from seizure, attachment, and the claims of creditors. While ongoing child support obligations may consider income sources, an unemployed, disabled veteran with zero non-exempt assets cannot be subjected to coercive incarceration threats or arbitrary cash purges derived solely from federally protected disability subsistence compensation.
* Furthermore, ordering an unemployed individual with verified service-connected disabilities to submit to mandatory daily manual labor job searches, day labor, and monthly logs—without conducting an evidentiary assessment of his medical impairments and physical capacity—violates basic principles of equity.

#### 5. Erroneous Arrears Baseline: Adoptions and the 2016 Marion Departure
* Paragraph 4 attempts to preserve the Marion County docket by asserting that enforcement relates strictly to arrears accumulated prior to December 19, 2017 (originally stated as \$11,220.81, though the current official DOR portal reflects an entirely disparate balance of \$3,848.67).
* This calculation is substantively tainted:
  1. **Legal Severance by Adoption (§ 63.172, Fla. Stat.):** Marion County’s pre-2017 numbers accrued on a baseline charging Respondent for **four children**. Two of those children—Noah and Charles—were legally adopted. Adoption extinguishes child support duties as a matter of law. Arrears derived from adopted children are legal nullities that cannot be grandfathered into a valid judgment.
  2. **Kelly Overton Permanently Vacated in February 2016:** Kelly Overton resided in Marion County only briefly and vacated no later than February 2016. Under *Department of Revenue v. Jackson*, 846 So. 2d 486 (Fla. 2003), child support is an equitable trust held solely for the children's daily care. Kelly Overton cannot retain or enforce arrears for periods when she maintained no household or custodial care in the Fifth Judicial Circuit.
  3. **The Brevard Decree Governs (§ 6, Exhibit D-2):** Circuit Judge Segal’s certified 2021 Brevard Final Judgment adjudicated all historical obligations back to **July 1, 2018**, naming Sharon Overton sole payee. Maintaining a parallel, conflicting Marion enforcement track creates duplicate IV-D enforcement prohibited under **45 C.F.R. § 303.11**.
