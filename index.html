Arcana Forensics

Evidence acquisition without the cloud.

An air-gapped digital forensics pipeline engineered in Rust. Acquire filesystem artifacts, seal them in an AES-256-GCM vault, and preserve a verifiable chain of custody.

View the source Read the runbook

Local-first acquisition

Scan approved filesystem paths with traversal protections and no outbound telemetry requirement.

Encrypted isolation

Derive keys with Argon2id and seal evidence using authenticated AES-256-GCM encryption.

Audit-ready integrity

Record SHA-256 hashes, signatures, timestamps, and vault metadata in a local SQLite ledger.

Arcana Forensics is software for authorized investigations. Preserve your own operational, legal, and evidence-handling procedures.
